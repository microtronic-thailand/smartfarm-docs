# User Manual

Welcome to the Smart Farm IoT Platform. This guide will help you navigate and master the dashboard to ensure your farm operates at peak efficiency.

---

## 🧭 Main Navigation

The sidebar on the left is your primary way to switch between features:
- **Overview**: A high-level summary of your entire farm status.
- **Live Monitor**: Real-time sensor data and historical trend analysis.
- **Smart Home**: Control home automation and indoor growing environments.
- **Marketplace**: Enhance your system with premium templates and extensions.
- **My Devices**: Manage hardware connections and device settings.

---

## 📊 Understanding the Dashboard

We use universal icons to provide instant feedback on your farm's health:

### 1. Sensor Status Cards
- 🌡️ **Temperature**: Monitor heat and cold levels (Unit: °C).
- 💧 **Humidity**: Relative air moisture (Unit: %).
- 🌱 **Soil Moisture**: How wet or dry the soil is (Unit: %).
- ☀️ **Light Level**: Check if plants are getting enough sunlight (Unit: Lux).

> **Pro Tip**: If a value turns red, it means the sensor reading is outside the safe range you've set.

### 2. Trend Charts
Visualizes changes over the last 24 hours, helping you identify when your crops might be under environmental stress.

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

Directly control field equipment from your screen:
- **Water Drop Icon (Irrigation)**: Toggle your watering system On/Off.
- **Sun Icon (Lights)**: Toggle grow lights On/Off.

---

## 🏠 Hybrid System (What if the Internet goes down?)

Our platform is designed for reliability:
1. **Online Mode**: All data is stored in the Cloud and accessible from anywhere.
2. **Offline Mode (Hybrid)**: If the internet fails, your controller automatically switches to the **Edge Hub** on-site to continue running your pre-set automation schedules.

---

## 🆘 Basic Troubleshooting

- **Red Status Light**: Device disconnected. Check power supply and WiFi signal.
- **Frozen Sensor Readings**: Check the physical wiring of the sensor to the board.
- **Display Issues**: Try clicking the **Refresh** button (circular arrow) at the top right.
